# Qottuf Aljazeera — Tier 1 by End-2028
### Roadmap v1.0 · 19 November 2026

*Agreed by the Chief Sales Officer and the Chief Marketing Officer in the executive meeting of 18–19 November 2026 (minutes: `meetings/2026-11-19-cso-cmo-executive-meeting.md`, item 3). Companion documents: `qottuf-business-development-department-v1.md` and `qottuf-weakness-register.md`.*

> **The answer, in one paragraph.** "Tier 1" means four different things, and a plan that doesn't say which can't be measured. By the end of 2028 we commit to two of them: being the first-call main contractor in our segment (**T1-Segment**), and a delivery record that proves it (**T1-Delivery**). We build toward a third, recognition by the major owners' programmes (**T1-Market**), through joint ventures whose Qottuf share fits our band. The fourth, Grade 1 classification (**T1-Formal**), is not reachable organically by 2028 from a Grade 3, SAR 20–100 m base. It goes to the shareholders as an explicit capital-or-partner decision, structured so that the grade sits in Qottuf's own name. Grade 1 is a licence for the government market. It isn't what Tier 1 means to the private and semi-private clients who gave Qottuf 8 of its 12 verified projects.

---

## 1. Four levels, each with its own test

| Level | What it means | How it is tested | Who owns it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1-Segment** | The first-call main contractor for Riyadh institutional and corporate buildings and fit-outs, SAR 20–100 m | Invitation rate and shortlist rate with the 20 priority organisations; documented references in band; unaided mentions in the annual recognition census | Business Development |
| **T1-Delivery** | Every project in band delivered so that it becomes a reference | Per project, from the tracker: programme variance at each milestone; HSE incidents; defects closed; payment certificates approved; client sign-off at handover. **Only projects that pass become references** | Company (PMO, delivery), shared with BD |
| **T1-Market** | Recognised by the major owners' programmes (PIF portfolio developers, top universities, hospital groups, banks) | Prequalification letters from those programmes; JV membership on projects above the band | BD (origination) + company (bonding, capital) |
| **T1-Formal** | Grade 1 classification in Construction & Building | The certificate, in Qottuf's own name | Shareholders and company |

Win rate is reported but not used as a test before 2028: with fewer than 30 decided pursuits, the department's own statistics rule calls movements noise.

---

## 2. Why Grade 1 isn't reachable organically by 2028

- Classification grades cap the value of **government** contracts; private owners prequalify on their own terms (references, financial capacity, bonding).
- Moving up a grade requires meeting the next grade's financial, staffing and executed-experience criteria, and experience at a higher level is earned by completing projects at that level. Two upgrades (Grade 3 → 2 → 1) in about 25 months would need a step change in capital and executed volume that commercial work alone can't produce. *(The exact criteria must be verified against the Contractor Classification Law's implementing regulation; public sources disagree even on Grade 3's own ceiling, quoting anywhere from SAR 30 m to 200 m.)*
- **What we do instead of saying "not possible":** state what each level costs, who pays and by when (sections 3–5).

## 3. Three routes

| Route | What it involves | Where it gets Qottuf by end-2028 | Conditions | Decides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Organic** | Win and deliver SAR 20–100 m main contracts; file the next grade when eligible | T1-Segment and T1-Delivery; the next grade filed or granted; full Tier 1 around 2030 or later | The gates in section 5 | Business Development + company |
| **B · JV-accelerated** (from 2027) | Join larger projects with a Grade 1 / Tier 1 partner, local or foreign, through the Muqawil Consortium Platform or bilateral talks | References inside SAR 150–500 m projects; visibility with major owners; a stronger case for the top grade | Qottuf's share counted against the band (30% of SAR 200 m = SAR 60 m); a defined scope delivered by Qottuf's own people; the evidence store records share and scope; never presented as Qottuf's alone; partner named only with consent; partner's disputes and payment record checked before signing | Asil |
| **C · Capital or partner** | Strategic investor, merger with a classified contractor, or a foreign contractor needing a Saudi partner | The fastest route to T1-Formal | Structured so that **Qottuf holds the classification and the delivery record**: a grade borrowed through someone else's licence wins work Qottuf can't deliver. Brand architecture decided before any announcement; strict attribution; clients, PMCs and staff told in person first; legal clearance on every disclosure; the evidence store becomes the data room; the partner gets the same reputation check as a JV partner | Shareholders |

**Recommendation:** run A now; prepare B from 2027; put C to the shareholders as an explicit decision instead of letting "Tier 1 by 2028" silently require it. **We say Grade 1 when the certificate in our name says it.**

**The pivot rule:** if the 17 December proof test fails (Qottuf can't produce its own documents for three projects), route B becomes the lead route from January: JV participation builds the references that route A assumes Qottuf already has.

## 7. The traps

1. **Over-reach.** A SAR 150 m project won without the people, bonding and systems to deliver it can end the reputation in one season. The gates make ambition follow capability.
2. **The first flagship goes wrong in public** (serious delay, dispute or site incident). In this band the same 20 owners and PMCs will see it, and the documented record is too short to absorb it. Choose the first pursuits for deliverability, reference value and payer reliability, not size. Have the crisis protocol in place by G1: the client hears from Asil first, in person; one spokesperson; holding statements approved in advance in both languages; nothing public before the client conversation; no agent ever answers publicly.
3. **A public claim contradicted during a review.** Live today ("13+ Years", the Ministry of Interior logo, "Fuji Japan"); removed by G0.
4. **A borrowed grade.** Route C structured so the grade and the record are Qottuf's own.

## 8. Measures that need design work

**Recognition census (annual, baseline Q1 2027):**
- run by an independent interviewer with no stake in the answers (a one-off researcher or a university research partner), with a small explicit budget and PDPL consent;
- a census, not a sample: all 20 priority organisations plus 20 that Qottuf has never worked with;
- the first question is unaided ("Which contractors would you invite to tender for a SAR 50 m institutional building in Riyadh?"); the aided questions follow; answers are recorded verbatim; the script stays fixed year to year;
- results are reported as counts (how many of the 20 targets name Qottuf unprompted), because at about 40 interviews percentages mislead;
- its most valuable output is the objections in the respondents' own words, which tell us which proof to build next.

**AI answer engines:** not a Tier 1 measure. A quarterly check of what they say about Qottuf by name decides only which public sources to correct.
